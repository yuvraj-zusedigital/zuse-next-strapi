# Blog posts

## Writing a post
1. Enter the **Title**; **Generate** creates the **slug** (the address: `/blog/your-slug`).
2. Write the content, add a cover **Image** and pick the **Author**.
3. Fill in **SEO** (title, description, share image) and check the score.
4. **Publish**. The post appears on the Blog page and, if featured, on the home page.

## AI tools (in the blog editor)
- **AI summary** writes a short summary of the post.
- **Audio** creates a narrated version with a choice of voices. Both have usage limits per hour, because they cost money.
