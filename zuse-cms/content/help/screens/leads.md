# Leads

Everything people send through the website. Only **Admins** can see this section, because it holds personal details.

## The three lists
- **Form submissions**: enquiries from the contact forms, with the message, budget, phone number and how they heard about us.
- **Subscribers**: people who signed up for updates on the blog.
- **Visitor tracking**: the pages each person viewed before they got in touch, and how long they spent on each one.

## Working with a lead
- Click a row to see everything about it, including the **visitor journey**: the pages they read, in order, grouped by day.
- **Email** and **Call** open your mail app or phone with their details filled in.
- **Export CSV** downloads the list (or just the records matching your search) for a spreadsheet or CRM.
- **Delete** removes a record permanently. Use it for spam, or when someone asks for their data to be removed. Deleting a submission also deletes its visitor journey.

## Good to know
- New submissions also arrive by email and sync to HubSpot, as before. This screen is the full record.
- The visitor journey is only recorded in the visitor's own browser until they send a form.
