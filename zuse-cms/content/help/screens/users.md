# Users

Everyone who can sign in to the CMS (Admins and Super Admins).

## Roles
- **Editor**: edits and publishes content and media.
- **Admin**: also deletes, edits site settings, sees the activity log and manages Editors.
- **Super Admin**: full access, including managing Admins.

## Managing accounts
- **Add user**: enter the email, pick a role and share the generated password securely.
- **Reset password**, **Deactivate** (signs them out straight away) and **Activate** are on each row.
- You can't change your own role or deactivate yourself, and there's always at least one Super Admin.
