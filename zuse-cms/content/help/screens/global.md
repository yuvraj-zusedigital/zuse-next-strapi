# Header & Footer

The site-wide menus and footer (Admins only).

## Header
- **Nav links** and the **Services** dropdown (**Service links**): each link can point to a **Page** (it follows the page if its path changes) or a typed **URL**.
- **Header CTA**: the button on the right, e.g. "Start a Project".

## Footer
- **Footer heading** and **Footer CTA**: the "Have a project in mind?" band. Wrap words in *asterisks* for gradient text.
- **Footer columns**: link columns such as Company, Services and Projects.
- **Office**, **Social links** and **Legal links**.

## Showing and hiding
- Every link, column and social icon has an **eye** button.
- The **Hide …** switches turn off whole blocks: the services menu, the call-to-action band, the description, the office and the social icons.
- A list you leave empty uses the built-in one; a list whose items are all hidden shows nothing.

Publish to apply. Page previews show the published header and footer.

## Copyright line
The line at the very bottom of the footer, before Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Write `{year}` for the current year so it never goes out of date (e.g. `© Zuse Digital {year}, All rights reserved.`). Text snippets like `{logo}` work too. Switch on **Hide copyright line** to remove it; the legal links stay. Publish to apply.
