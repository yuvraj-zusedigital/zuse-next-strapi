# Dashboard

An overview of everything on the site.

- **Headline figures**: published entries, unpublished changes, drafts and edits in the last 7 days (compared with the 7 days before, with a small trend line). Admins also see new enquiries.
- **Content by type**: one bar per content type, split into Published, Unpublished changes and Draft. Hover or tab to a bar for its count; click it to open that list, filtered to that status.
- **Edits per day**: everything saved, published or uploaded in the CMS over the last 30 days, counted in your time zone.
- **Enquiries per week** (Admins): contact form submissions over the last 12 weeks.
- Every chart has **Show as table** for the exact figures.
- **New page**, **New blog post** and **Upload media** are shortcuts to the most common tasks.
- The clock (top right) shows your saved time zone. Click it to change the time zone.
- Top right: **Help**, the clock and your initials. Click your initials to see your email and role, and to **Sign out**.
- Dark mode and the **Colour theme** dropdown (10 themes) are at the bottom of the sidebar. The colour theme is saved to your account (it follows you to any device); dark mode is remembered per browser.
