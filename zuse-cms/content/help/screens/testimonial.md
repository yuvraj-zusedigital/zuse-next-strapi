# Testimonials

Client quotes, used on the Testimonials page, the home page carousel, Work and Awards.

- **Content**: the full quote. Separate paragraphs with a blank line.
- **Excerpt**: a short version for the carousels. Leave empty to use the first paragraph.
- **Portrait** and **Logo**: the client's photo and company logo. **Logo width** fine-tunes the logo size.
- **Rank**: the order everywhere (lower first). Unpublish a testimonial to remove it from all pages.
- A testimonials section with no testimonials picked shows every published one.
