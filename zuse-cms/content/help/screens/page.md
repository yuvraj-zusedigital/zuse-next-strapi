# Pages

A page is built from **sections**: blocks such as a hero, a services grid or testimonials, shown on the site in the order listed.

## Building a page
1. Give the page a **Name** (only used in the CMS) and a **Path**, its address on the site, e.g. `/careers`.
2. Click **Add section** (or the **+** that appears between sections) and pick a section type.
3. Fill in the section's fields. Drag the handle, or use the arrows, to reorder sections.
4. **Publish** when it's ready. A new page with a path is live at that address straight away.

## Useful tools on each section card
- **Eye**: hide the section on the site without deleting it. It stays in the CMS, faded in the preview.
- **Copy**: duplicate the section, e.g. to reuse its layout.
- **?**: explains what that section type does.

## Good to know
- Most built-in pages (Home, About, Services…) keep their own look; sections still decide the content and the order.
- If every section is hidden, the page shows only the header and footer.
- Fill in **SEO** so the page has a proper Google title, description and share image. The SEO panel scores it and suggests fixes.

## Draft with AI
**Draft with AI** (next to **New page**) writes a first draft from a short brief: who the page is for, what you offer, the key points and what visitors should do next.

- You get an **unpublished draft** with an intro, text sections (benefits, steps, offerings), an FAQ and a call to action, plus the SEO title, description and focus keyword.
- It only uses text sections. Add images, case studies, testimonials and the SEO image yourself.
- Check every claim before publishing; the AI is told not to invent facts, but it can still get things wrong.
- Drafts are limited to 20 per person per hour. The button is greyed out until an OpenAI key is set up in Strapi.

## Hiding a page from Google
In the page's **SEO** block, set **Search engines** to **Hide from search results**. The page stays on the site (people with the link can still open it), but Google won't show it and it's left out of the sitemap. The same setting works on blog posts and projects (case studies).
