# Getting started

Zuse CMS manages the content of zusedigital.com. Everything you edit here is saved as a **draft** until you **publish** it.

## The basics
- **Save draft** keeps your changes without showing them on the site. With **Autosave** on, drafts save a few seconds after you stop typing.
- **Publish** puts the current draft live. The site updates on the next page load.
- **Unpublish** takes an entry off the site; **Discard changes** throws away an unpublished draft.
- The preview on the right is approximate and updates as you type.

## Finding your way
- **Content** in the sidebar lists everything you can edit: pages, blog posts, projects, testimonials and more.
- **Site settings** (Admins) holds the Header & Footer and organisation details.
- Press **?** on any screen to open the guide for that screen.

## Text snippets
- Write `{phone}`, `{year}` or any snippet from **Site settings → Text snippet** in a text, and the site shows its current value once the page is published.

## Working with lists
- Filter by status: **Draft** (never published), **Published**, or **Modified** (live, with changes not yet published).
- Click the **Title** or **Last updated** heading to sort; click again to reverse.
- Tick entries to publish, unpublish or export them together. Admins can also delete them.
- **Export CSV** downloads every entry that matches the current search and filter.
