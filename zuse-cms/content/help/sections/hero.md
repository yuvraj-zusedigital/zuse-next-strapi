# Hero

The big opening of a page: a short **label**, a large **title**, a **description** and up to two buttons.

- In the **title**, wrap words in *asterisks* for gradient text; a line break starts a new line.
- A line break in the **label** only applies on phones.
- **Primary** and **secondary CTA** are the buttons; each has an eye to hide it.
- **Background image** sits behind the text.
