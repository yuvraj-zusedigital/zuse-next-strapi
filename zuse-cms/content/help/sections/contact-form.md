# Contact form

The enquiry form, with the page's quote slider beside it. Enquiries arrive by email and under **Leads → Form submissions**.

- **Title** and **description** sit above the form; in the description, **double asterisks** make text bold.
- **Fields**: each has a **label** (above the field) and a **placeholder** (grey hint inside it). Leave either empty to keep the original wording.
- **Phone, service, budget and "how did you hear about us?"** can be made optional (switch **Required** off) or removed from the form with the eye button. Name, email and message are always asked for.
- **Service, budget and referral options**: one option per line.
- **"Stay updated" text**: the consent line under the form. Line breaks are kept, `**bold**` and `[link text](/privacy-policy)` work. Choose whether the box starts ticked.
- **Button text** and **error message** (shown if the form can't be sent).
- **Success path**: the page shown after sending, usually `/thank-you`.
- **Thank-you title**: write `{name}` where the visitor's first name should go.
