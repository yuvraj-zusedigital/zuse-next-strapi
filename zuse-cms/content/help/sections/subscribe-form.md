# Subscribe box

A newsletter sign-up with name and email, shown at the foot of the Blog page. It can be added to other pages too. Sign-ups appear under **Leads → Subscribers**.

- **Title** and **description**: the text beside the form.
- **Placeholders**: the grey hints inside the name and email fields.
- **Require first and last name**: on, visitors must enter both.
- **Button text**, **thank-you title and text** (they replace the form once someone signs up) and the **error message**.
- Leave any field empty to keep the original wording. Use the eye button to hide the box without deleting it.
